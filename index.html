Quick How-To Examples

Parents: these show the thinking process, not just the answer.

How do I interpret a positive or negative integer?
First identify the reference point, usually zero. A positive integer represents a value above, greater than, or in the positive direction from zero. A negative integer represents a value below, less than, or in the opposite direction from zero. Always explain what the sign means in the context.
How do I compare integers?
Picture the values on a number line. The number farther to the right is greater. For example, −2 is greater than −7 because −2 lies farther right on the number line.
What does zero mean?
Zero is the reference point. Its meaning depends on the situation: sea level, no gain or loss, no money owed, or a starting temperature. Students should identify that meaning before interpreting positive and negative values.
