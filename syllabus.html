Course Information & Family Guide

The essentials for a successful year in Mr. Martin's math class—expectations, routines, grading scale, materials, classroom rules, and family support.

A Note About Math

Everyone can do math. Struggle is part of learning. Practice, mistakes, questions, and trying again help students build stronger understanding. Families can help by encouraging a positive attitude toward math, even when the work feels difficult.

How Math Class Works

📘 Math Every Day

New topics take practice. Homework will be assigned, and students will show their thinking in written, verbal, and visual ways.

📝 Notebook + Online

Maintain a working notebook, check Google Classroom daily, and come prepared with needed materials.

📅 Due Dates Matter

Most work is due on the date given. Late work is not accepted. After an absence, make-up work is due within 5 days of returning.

Grading Scale

✏️ Homework & Absences

Homework reinforces current learning, previews upcoming work, or finishes incomplete classwork. Students are responsible for make-up work after an absence and have 5 days from their return.

💻 Google Classroom

Students should check Google Classroom daily for posted assignments, resources, and class information.

✅ Academic Honesty

Collaboration is encouraged, but plagiarism and cheating are not collaboration. Students must complete their own work in their own words. Academic dishonesty may result in a zero and an office referral.

🏠 Helping at Home

Ask your student to explain how they know—not only what the answer is. Encourage questions, persistence, and trying again when a problem is challenging.
