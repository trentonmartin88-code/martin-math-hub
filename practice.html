Check answers after you finish
1. −85

3. −8, −3, 0, 2, 6

7. Quadrant II
8. (−3, −6)
9. −12 because |−12| = 12 and |9| = 9.
10. −13; the negative sign represents movement in the losing direction.

Check possible answers
