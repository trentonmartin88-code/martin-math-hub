Lesson 3-5 Practice: Evaluate Algebraic Expressions

Substitute carefully, then use the order of operations. The questions move from direct evaluation to reasoning and application.

Before you calculate:

Write the substituted expression first. This catches many mistakes before they happen.

2 • Whole NumbersDOK 1

3 • DecimalsDOK 1

4 • FractionsDOK 1

Evaluate ¾p + 2 when p = 8.

5 • ExponentsDOK 2

6 • Two VariablesDOK 2

Evaluate 5a + 2b when a = 3 and b = 4.

7 • Real-World ExpressionDOK 2

A rental costs $9.50 per hour plus a $6 fee. The total cost is represented by 9.50h + 6. Find the cost for h = 4 and explain what each part of the expression represents.

8 • Error AnalysisDOK 2

A student evaluates 4 + 3x for x = 5 by adding 4 + 3 first and then multiplying by 5. Explain the error and give the correct value.

9 • Compare ValuesDOK 3

For the expression 2.5n + 3, Student A uses n = 4 and Student B uses n = 6. Find both values. Then explain why increasing n by 2 changes the expression's value by exactly 5.

10 • Structure & ReasoningDOK 3

Two expressions are 4x + 7 and 2x + 15. Evaluate both when x = 3 and when x = 5. Which expression is greater each time? Explain what changed.

Lesson 3-5 Answer Key
1. 27
2. 14
3. 28.25
4. 8
5. 13; evaluate 2² = 4 before multiplying by 3.
6. 23
7. $44.00; 9.50h is the hourly cost and 6 is the fixed fee.
8. The student changed the order of operations. Correct: 4 + 3(5) = 4 + 15 = 19.
9. A: 13; B: 18. Because n increases by 2 and the coefficient is 2.5, the expression increases by 2.5 × 2 = 5.
10. x = 3: both equal 19. x = 5: 4x + 7 = 27 and 2x + 15 = 25, so 4x + 7 is greater. The first expression grows faster because its coefficient of x is larger.
